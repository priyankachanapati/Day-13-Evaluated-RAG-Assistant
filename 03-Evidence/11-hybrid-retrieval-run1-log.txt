## 2. Relevance and metric definitions

- **Relevant chunk**: fully contains at least one required canonical fact (evidence ID) of the query. Same rule for every metric, strategy and method.
- **Hit@K**: a relevant chunk is in the top K. **RR@K**: 1/rank of the first relevant chunk in the top K (0 if none); **MRR** = mean RR over the 5 queries.
- **P@K** (conventional): relevant chunks in top K ÷ K. **R@K** (conventional, chunk-level): relevant chunks in top K ÷ relevant chunks in the index — depends on the chunking (overlap duplicates enlarge it), so it is **not** comparable across strategies.
- **Coverage@K** (evidence facts): required fact groups satisfied by the top-K chunks ÷ groups. Each fact counts once even if several retrieved chunks contain it (no double counting from overlap). This is the cross-strategy measure.
