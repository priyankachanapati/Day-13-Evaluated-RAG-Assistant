**Score types are kept separate.** *RRF* (before) only encodes the ranks a chunk got from dense and BM25 (≈ 0.014–0.033). The *cross-encoder logit* (after) is the model's relevance estimate for one (query, chunk) pair; it is unbounded, can be negative, and is used **only to order the 10 candidates**. Neither score is a cosine similarity: the v1 refusal gate stays on the dense similarity (≥ 0.25), and no cross-encoder threshold is introduced.

- **Pool coverage** = Coverage@10 of the candidate pool. Reranking only re-orders the pool, so pool coverage is identical before and after; it is the upper bound for what reranking can deliver.

## 3. Per-query top-3 before and after reranking
